v The previous methods of interpolation concerned the approximation of 
arbitrary functions on closed intervals using a single polynomial. 
v However, high-degree polynomials can oscillate erratically, that is, a 
minor fluctuation over a small portion of the interval can induce large 
fluctuations over the entire range.

v An alternative approach to avoid large number of fluctuations arising 
from the behavior of higher degree polynomials and minor fluctuation 
of original data is what we refer to as piecewise-polynomial 
approximation.
ü An alternative approach divides the approximation interval into a 
collection of subintervals and construct a (generally) different 
approximating polynomial on each subinterval

polynomials between each successive pair of nodes and is called cubic 
spline interpolation.
